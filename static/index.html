Features

Built for a desk that cannot afford a bad fill

Gmail Primary Inbox Filtering

Only Primary-inbox mail reaches the negotiation graph. Promotions, Social, Updates, Spam and Trash are skipped, and every message is claimed exactly once by Message-ID.

Autonomous SCO Generation

Soft corporate offers, counters and confirmations are drafted around numbers the pricing engine already computed, with a template fallback for every draft.

CIF Margin Protection

Landed cost, FOB ceiling and CIF floor are deterministic arithmetic against live benchmarks. The language model never prices or judges a deal.

HITL Approval

Every viable deal pauses at a human approval gate. On approval, supplier allocation locks first and the buyer is confirmed second.

Workflow

From inbound email to locked margin

  1. 01ParseExtract price, volume, incoterm and port from the email
  2. 02PricePull FOB benchmarks and freight for the lane
  3. 03EvaluateScore the spread against the hard floor and soft target
  4. 04CounterCounter the buyer or supplier, or decline
  5. 05Approve & lockA human signs off, then supplier and buyer lock in that order
